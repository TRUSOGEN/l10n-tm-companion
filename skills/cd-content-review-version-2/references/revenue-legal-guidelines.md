3. This document is not a substitute for legal review. All content for each request should be reviewed by a legal POC.

# Legal principles & workarounds 法务大原则与推荐写法

1. **Avoid relating Gifts directly with money //避免将礼物与金钱直接联系起来***Here's how gifting is defined in our Virtual Items Policy*

Gifting:

- is a way to **express your personality and appreciation for the content** with a unique or specialised reaction; and

- allows you to**interact with the content you enjoy**.

- Gifting can unlock the creator's eligibility to receive rewards, but TikTok looks at several factors to determine whether a creator is eligible for rewards and in what amount, including performance metrics and content quality.

| **Do ✓** | **Don't ✕**|
|---|---|
| The more Gifts sent in your LIVE, the more Diamonds you could collect. | The more Gifts your LIVE receives, the more rewards you'll get. |

- The word or icon for Diamonds shouldn't be positioned on a page so that a user might understand them to be a reward or equate to cash.

This is true because Diamonds are measurement of a creator's popularity. It is a symbol but not rewards. Thus Diamonds should be singled out when they are discussed together with rewards.

We typically recommend this language to be added to the campaign UI where Diamonds are used: "In campaigns you may collect diamonds as a measurement to indicate your engagement and popularity. Separately, TikTok will reward you based on your performance in campaigns."

| **Do ✓** | **Don't ✕**|
|---|---|
| Rewards and Diamonds | Campaign rewards 1. Diamonds |

- Avoid expressions like "buy Gifts", "spend $x on Gifts", or "the Gift costs $x", which imply that Gifts have monetary value. Instead, users purchase Coins first and use them to send Gifts.

| **Do ✓** | **Don't ✕**|
|---|---|
| Set a daily Coin max to manage the number of Coins you can use to send Gifts daily. | Set a daily Gift budget to manage the amount you spend to send Gifts daily. |
| Save 25% on Coins you can use to send your favorite Gift | Your favorite Gift costs 25% less on tiktok.com |

1. **Always clearly state the actual cost of an action //务必清楚地说明动作所需的实际成本**- When users are sending Gifts with points, do not include a price with Coins in the action button. If a Gift is sent out at no cost, there can be no price indication in the action button.

| **Do ✓** | **Don't ✕**|
|---|---|
| You can encourage viewers to send a Heart Me if they like your LIVE. | Encourage viewers to send a Heart Me if they like your LIVE. |

- According to Consumer Protection Cooperation, if users can access a feature and use Coins bypass the LIVE Gifts panel, ensure users in European Economic Area countries (EEA), UK, and Switzerland are aware of the Coin price during the user journey.

| **Do ✓** | **Don't ✕**|
|---|---|

1. **Give fair representation without overpromising //公正描述，不要过度承诺**Don't promise that using a feature will guarantee a certain result. Instead, let users know that it will increase their chances of a possible outcome.

| **Do ✓** | **Don't ✕**|

| Select interests for a better chance of finding a like-minded creator | Select interests to find a similar creator |

1. **Avoid dark patterns in both UX and content //避免设计和内容中的“黑暗模式”**Dark patterns are manipulative design or writing techniques that trick users into doing things they might not want to do.

- Avoid asymmetric choice in retention modals Place even emphasis on both choices, avoid using different background colors, text size, or contrast.
- The primary button should align with the user's intended choice, eg, if the user selects "Cancel", the primary button should be "Yes, cancel".

- Make sure users can unsubscribe as easily as they do to subscribe

Eg., if users can complete the subscription in 2 steps, they must be able to unsubscribe in 2 steps as well.

# Sub-vertical guidelines 细分原则

## Strategy 策略

1. **Use only "Send" as the CTA in terms of Gifts for accuracy **When the button indicates users are sending Gifts to a LIVE, the button should always be "Send" instead of vague expressions like "Support" or "Continue".

| **Do ✓** | **Don't ✕**|
|---|---|
| Send (🪙 1) | Support (🪙 1) |

## System 体系

1. **Do not directly tell creators to perform certain actions **We should avoid orderly language. Instead, use words like "You can do something" or "Try doing something" to make it sound more like a suggestion.

| **Do ✓** | **Don't ✕**|
|---|---|
| You can encourage viewers to send a Heart Me if they like your LIVE. | Encourage viewers to send a Heart Me if they like your LIVE. |
| Try adding a sticker to your LIVE. It may rally your fans to help you climb up Popular LIVE! | Add a sticker to your LIVE to rally your fans to help you rank up on Popular LIVE. |

1. **Do not speak on behalf of the users **Content appearing as part of the UI shall always take the perspective of the platform.

| **Do ✓** | **Don't ✕**|
|---|---|
| Thank you for being a Super Fan! | Thank you for being my Super Fan! |

## Wallet 钱包

1. **LIVE rewards that are not final should be called "estimated rewards"**Always add "estimated" or the short form "est." before the amount, until it's finalized and updated on the creator's LIVE rewards page.

| **Do ✓** | **Don't ✕**|
|---|---|
| Before LIVE During LIVE After LIVE | - Leave the amount alone without "est.". |

1. **Describe fixed-rate cash back offers in the form of "x% cash back up to $x"**Show both the percentage and cap for clarity.

| **Do ✓** | **Don't ✕**|
|---|---|
| Recharge to get 5% cash back up to $150! | Recharge to get cash back up to $150! |

1. **Clearly state the conditions for tiered cash back offers**- State the conditions for each tier if possible. If it's too long for the UI, make sure users know the minimum spend and the cash back range.

| **Do ✓** | **Don't ✕**|
|---|---|
| Up to $10 cash back: spend $35 to get $10, $20 to get $8, or $8 to get $5. | Up to $10 cash back: spend more to get more. |
| Spend min. $8 to get cash back from $5 to $10. | Spend min. $8 to get cash back up to $10. |

- Add time limit if there is one (e.g., "spend $35 in the next 7 days to get $10 cash back"), or if it has to be in one purchase (e.g., "spend at least $35 in a single purchase in the next 7 days to get $10 cash back").

1. **Give users a 30-day prior notice on feature updates **Oftentimes, the product doesn't wish to share the specific date because they want to have more space for potential changes. In that case, we should at least say "xxx will be updated soon" or "we'll soon xxx" to give users a sense of when the change will happen.

| **Do ✓** | **Don't ✕**|
|---|---|
| TikTok LIVE will soon reward creators in a new way with Scaled LIVE Rewards. | TikTok LIVE will reward creators in a new way with Scaled LIVE Rewards. |

1. **Be cautious when using "every"**Use more general wordings if there are exceptions, or narrow the subject down to "every eligible xxx".

| **Do ✓** | **Don't ✕**|
|---|---|
| Set a default payout method and get auto payout on Mondays | Set a default payout method and get auto payout every Monday If the user's rewards are below the min. payout amount, they won't be paid the following Monday. |
| Once an eligible friend recharges, you’ll get cash back on their Coin purchases for the next 7 days. | Once a friend recharges, you’ll get cash back on their Coin purchases for the next 7 days. Only friends aged 18 or above are eligible for recharging. |

## Gift 礼物

### Viewer Wishes 观众心愿

** Avoid content-on-demand inferences **Since TikTok is not a content-on-demand platform, we should not infer that viewers are in control of what content is performed in the LIVE. It should be clear that creators are in control of the LIVE at all time and can choose whether to perform any content.

| **Do ✓** | **Don't ✕**|
|---|---|
| Viewer Wishes (new feature name) | Viewer Picks (old feature name) |
| Creators are not required to perform any particular type of content even if it receives the most points. | Creators will perform the content type that receives the most Gifts.|

** Clarify the meaning of gifting **Since TikTok is not a content-on-demand platform, we should not infer that gifting is an act of demanding a type of content, rather that is is only the act of expressing preference for a type of content.

| **Do ✓** | **Don't ✕**|
|---|---|
| Viewer Picks is a feature that allows viewers to indicate which types of LIVE content they want to see most by sending Gifts to the LIVE that correspond to their preferred type of content. | Viewer Wishes is a feature that allows viewers to pick/choose/request/demand content by sending a corresponding Gift to the LIVE. |

## Ranking and Interactions 互动和榜单

### Co-host 直播连麦

** Avoid references to pairing by TikTok's algorithm **To avoid any concerns about algorithm manipulation, avoid any language like 'pairing' or 'matching' that refers to TikTok's algorithm when referring to creators being randomly paired with other creators to co-host.

| **Do ✓** | **Don't ✕**|
|---|---|
| Quick invites (new feature name) | Quick pairing (old feature name) |

### LIVE match/Catch Beans/Take the Stage 直播PK/接萌豆/轮流登场

** Avoid displaying high scores **To avoid incentivizing excessive gifting, LIVE match scores over 1M should not be fully displayed.

| **Do ✓** | **Don't ✕**|
|---|---|
| 1M+ points | 1.9M points|

** Avoid creating urgency around gifting **LIVE matches are short, fast-paced, and intense, with creators hoping to collect as many points from likes and Gifts as they can within the 5-minute period. This can be a danger zone for dark patterns related to urgency, where viewers may feel pressured to send Gifts due to a time limit. Don't use any language to create urgency around gifting, especially during a bonus mission where a 30-second countdown is displayed.

| **Do ✓** | **Don't ✕**|
|---|---|
| Bonus mission (new feature name) | Speed challenge (old feature name) |
| x2 gifting points (30s) | Be quick, only 15s left to contribute x2 gifting points!|

** Provide sufficient info in gifting scenarios **In bonus missions, viewers have a short window of time in which they can send Gifts to complete the missions, after which the creator will have a short window of time to collect double or triple points. Always display the rules of the bonus mission clearly so that the viewers can make an informed decision before gifting.

| **Do ✓** | **Don't ✕**|
|---|---|
| Creators unlock ×3 gifting points for 30s after reaching the gifting point target. | LIVE creators unlock x3 gifting points for reaching the gifting point target.|

** Explain points logic clearly **Viewers should be aware of how many points they can contribute before taking action to do so, especially in the case of gifting. Always be precise when explaining the calculation method so that they can make an informed choice. If there are any caps, they should be stated clearly.

| **Do ✓** | **Don't ✕**|
|---|---|
| Points are calculated from comments, likes, and Coins used to send Gifts. 1 like = 3 points (max. 1 like counted per viewer) | Points are calculated from Gifts, comments, and likes. 1 like = 3 points|

** Avoid describing match power-ups as prizes **Match power-ups are randomly distributed to the top 3 contributors in a match. Since not every top 3 contributor will get a power-up every time they make the top 3, avoid giving viewers the impression that contributors will definitely get match power-ups, or that match power-ups are prizes that are given for sending Gifts.

| **Do ✓** | **Don't ✕**|
|---|---|
| Top 3 contributors may get match power-ups. | Top 3 contributors will be awarded with match power-ups. |

### LIVE Talent 直播达人

** Remind creators of copyright violation risk **As our platform should adhere to copyright law at all times, we must remind creators in the LIVE Talent program who produce music content to obtain the legal rights to perform anyone else's music.

**Add to requirements page and FAQ ✓**|
|---|
| You may only play or perform music you have the rights and permissions to play on TikTok LIVE.

** Avoid employment risk regarding music creators **The LIVE Talent music program gives creators a chance to get profile labels for producing music LIVE streams to a high-quality standard. The program is a channel for creators to further their music pursuits, but we should avoid any phrasing that implies that creators can have or improve their music career through TikTok LIVE, as this carries employment risk.

| **Do ✓** | **Don't ✕**|
|---|---|
| Find your pathway to grow and make your contribution to LIVE music on TikTok. | We offer a pathway for singers and musicians to grow and develop their careers on TikTok LIVE. |

### Group LIVE 团播

** Clarify the meaning of gifting **When multiple creators are LIVE together in Group LIVE, they can be individually selected on the gifting panel. As viewers are able to select a specific creator on the gifting panel while gifting, it is important to make it clear that selecting a creator is not for the purpose of sending the Gift to them, but that it is for expressing appreciation for that specific creator's performance in the Group LIVE.

**Add to FAQ ✓**|
|---|
| All Gifts are sent to the Group LIVE, but you can show appreciation for specific members’ performances by selecting specific members when you send Gifts.

** Be clear about where the rewards are going **Since the account used to go LIVE is owned by the Creator Network, all revenue accrued from the Group LIVE will go to the Creator Network, after which they can reward creators as they wish. Make it clear that selecting members on the gifting panel does not mean that any rewards will go to that creator accordingly, and that all rewards will go to the group account hosting the LIVE.

**Add to FAQ ✓**|
|---|
| You can send Gifts to the Group LIVE to show appreciation for content. The group account hosting the LIVE may receive rewards from TikTok subject to our Rewards Policy.

** Clarify the meaning of points **In Group LIVE, individual members collect points from gifting in the same way as creators in LIVE match. Clarify that points are a popularity metric only, even if the winner does not get any real benefits other than honor.

| **Do ✓** | **Don't ✕**|
|---|---|
| 1 vs 1 performances are performances in Group LIVE that let 2 group members perform at a time and collect points to show their popularity. Points will be collected based on the rules below, and the member with the most points will be the winner. | 1 vs 1 performances are performances in Group LIVE that let 2 group members perform at a time. The 2 members will collect points based on the rules below, and the member with the most points will be the winner.|

** Avoid emphasizing competition **Since the concept of competition may be fierce and could potentially be interpreted in a negative way by the public, avoid emphasizing competition and try to emphasise the performative or friendly nature of the feature instead.

| **Do ✓** | **Don't ✕**|
|---|---|
| 1 vs 1 performances are performances in Group LIVE that let 2 group members perform at a time. | 1 vs 1 performances are competitions in Group LIVE that let 2 group members compete against each other. |
